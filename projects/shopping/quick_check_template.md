# Quick Check Template

## Purpose
Define the compact presentation contract for `quick_check.md`. This file renders the workflow's narrow conclusion.

## Direct answer
Answer the user's narrow question immediately: suitable/not suitable, strong/weak deal, compatible/not compatible, or the relevant decision boundary.

## Confidence
State High / Medium / Low when useful, tied to the evidence actually checked.

## Key reasons
Give **2-4** decision-changing reasons. Do not pad with minor observations.

## Primary caveat / unknown
State the most important unresolved issue, or say none is material.

## Price / seller
When the workflow includes a concrete-offer or deal conclusion, render the decision-relevant price/seller note.

## Broader research
When the workflow escalates or recommends broader research, render that note briefly. Otherwise omit it.

## Formatting
- Keep the answer compact.
- Do not include a comparison table unless the workflow supplied an outside comparable that genuinely helps explain the judgment.
- Do not expose internal decision-question counts, research mode, or source-count mechanics unless audited.

## Boundary
This template owns presentation only. `quick_check.md` owns scope, decision-question limits, outside comparables, escalation, and conclusion logic.
