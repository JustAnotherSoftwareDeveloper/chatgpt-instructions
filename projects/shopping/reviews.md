# Review Interpretation Authority

## Purpose
Own interpretation of expert reviews, individual reviewers, owner reviews, retailer reviews, forums, and community evidence after `research_sources.md` establishes that the source can contribute to the claim.

This file does not set general evidence admissibility or seller/channel policy.

## 1) What review evidence is good for
Use review evidence to understand:
- hands-on performance and usability;
- long-term ownership behavior;
- recurring failure or QC patterns;
- setup/maintenance friction;
- preference-sensitive fit;
- edge cases that primary documentation does not capture.

Do not treat review sentiment or star averages as direct measurements of product quality.

## 2) Evaluate review evidence on these dimensions
### Identity
Can the review be tied to the correct product, model, generation, size, region, firmware, or revision when those distinctions matter?

Ambiguous identity weakens or invalidates variant-sensitive claims.

### Domain competence
Does the reviewer demonstrate the knowledge needed for the claims being made?

A generalist may be useful for broad usability while being weak evidence for niche technical conclusions.

### Method and specificity
Prefer reviews that show:
- concrete test/use conditions;
- direct comparisons;
- measurements or reproducible observations when applicable;
- exact failure modes rather than adjectives;
- duration/intensity of ownership when long-term claims are made.

### Independence and incentives
Consider:
- affiliate links;
- sponsorships;
- free samples;
- retailer/manufacturer relationships;
- undisclosed or unclear material connections;
- selective comparison sets that favor a sponsor or inventory.

An incentive does not automatically invalidate original evidence. It increases the need for transparency, concrete support, and independent corroboration.

### Time horizon
Match the evidence horizon to the claim.

Day-one reviews are weak evidence for durability, battery aging, coating wear, edge retention over repeated sharpening, or long-term software stability.

### Corroboration
Repeated independent observations matter more than one vivid anecdote.

Prefer patterns that recur across unrelated reviewers, owners, platforms, or evidence types.

## 3) Source-specific handling
### Method-based publications
Useful when the outlet has category competence, repeatable methods, and genuine comparative context.

Discount:
- affiliate roundups with little original work;
- shallow category coverage;
- conclusions broader than the test conditions;
- nominal "testing" with no useful methodology.

### Specialist individuals / creators
Useful for depth, edge cases, repair/sharpening/service knowledge, and categories where individuals outperform institutional coverage.

Look for:
- stable comparison methodology;
- concrete evidence;
- correct terminology and mental model;
- willingness to discuss downsides and uncertainty;
- clear disclosure of incentives.

Discount certainty theater, sponsored comparison framing, or claims unsupported by shown experience.

### Owner / retailer reviews
Useful for:
- recurring defects;
- QC variance;
- longevity;
- workflow friction;
- packaging/fulfillment problems;
- maintenance burden.

Weight detailed ownership reports far above generic praise/complaints.

Do not infer prevalence from the number of complaints without a meaningful denominator or representative dataset.

### Forums / communities
Useful for:
- niche expertise;
- troubleshooting;
- long-term experience;
- newly emerging issues;
- category vocabulary and hidden constraints.

Separate first-hand reports from speculation, repetition, folklore, and preference norms.

## 4) Manipulation and low-quality patterns
### Exclude or strongly discount when supported by evidence
- duplicated or near-duplicated review text across supposedly independent accounts;
- obvious synthetic/copied content with contradictory or hallucinated product facts;
- undisclosed incentivized reviews presented as independent;
- content laundering or paraphrase-only pages with no original evidence;
- reviews of the wrong model/variant used for variant-sensitive claims;
- technical claims from sources with neither competence nor method/context.

### Discount, but do not automatically exclude
- affiliate monetization with otherwise substantial original work;
- brand-provided samples with clear disclosure;
- strong enthusiasm or negativity when the underlying observations remain concrete;
- short ownership duration for claims that are genuinely short-term.

Writing style alone, including "AI-ish" prose, is weak evidence of manipulation. Prioritize duplication, identity, behavior, incentives, and factual quality.

## 5) Pattern detection
Treat a recurring issue as decision-relevant when substantially similar observations appear independently and the reports are specific enough to refer to the same phenomenon.

Strengthen confidence when one or more are true:
- the pattern spans different platforms or source types;
- evidence artifacts exist, such as measurements, photos, logs, or consistent physical symptoms;
- a competent reviewer reproduces the issue;
- the manufacturer acknowledges, repairs, revises, recalls, or otherwise responds to it.

Use language such as:
- isolated report;
- recurring owner reports;
- consistent cross-source pattern;
- widespread discussion but unclear prevalence;
- official acknowledgement.

Do not invent failure percentages.

## 6) Channel contamination
A review may describe the seller/channel rather than the product.

Possible channel signals include:
- wrong item or variant;
- opened/used product sold as new;
- missing accessories;
- suspected counterfeit;
- shipping damage;
- seller return/RMA problems.

Do not count these as product-quality failures until the distinction is reasonably clear. Route channel conclusions to `seller_instructions.md`.

## 7) Preference vs defect
In enthusiast or subjective categories, distinguish:
- objective defect or inconsistency;
- measurable tradeoff;
- maintenance expectation;
- technique mismatch;
- personal preference;
- community norm.

A knife being too light, too reactive, too flat, or too laser-like may be an advantage for one user and a drawback for another. Preserve the observation and map it to the user's preferences instead of converting it into universal quality.

## 8) Synthesis
When review evidence materially affects the recommendation:
- summarize the recurring pattern, not individual drama;
- note important disagreement or selection bias;
- distinguish what is well established from what is merely plausible;
- connect the pattern to a concrete user consequence.

Normal outputs should not include a review-manipulation report unless it materially changes the decision. `audit.md` may expose deeper review-evidence analysis.

## Boundary
`research_sources.md` owns general source admissibility, coverage, independence, and confidence; `seller_instructions.md` owns seller/channel risk; hierarchy authorities own product-domain interpretation of the observed behavior.
