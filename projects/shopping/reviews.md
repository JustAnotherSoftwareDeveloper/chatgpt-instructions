# Review Interpretation Authority

## Purpose
Own interpretation of expert, owner, retailer, forum, and community review evidence once `research_sources.md` has established that the source is usable.

## Initial stub
- Prefer repeated independent patterns over vivid single anecdotes.
- Distinguish product-quality complaints from seller/shipping complaints.
- Weight concrete model/version/use-context observations above generic sentiment.
- Account for affiliate incentives, selection effects, platform bias, and suspiciously repetitive review behavior.
- Treat enthusiast preference as useful context, not universal truth.

## Next expansion
Migrate detailed manipulation, astroturf, review-quality, and repeated-signal heuristics from the legacy implementation.

## Boundary
General source admissibility belongs to `research_sources.md`; seller/channel evaluation belongs to `seller_instructions.md`.
