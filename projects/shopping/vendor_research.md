# Vendor Research Workflow

## Purpose
Own research about a brand, manufacturer, retailer, or vendor ecosystem rather than one specific product recommendation.

## Inputs
Use the resolved product context when the vendor request belongs to a known product domain. Apply the same shared evidence and specialized authorities as relevant.

## Initial workflow stub
- Identify what kind of vendor is being evaluated: manufacturer/brand, specialist retailer, marketplace seller, or broader ecosystem.
- Research product positioning, strengths/weaknesses, support/distribution, reputation evidence, and domain-specific fit.
- Keep brand/product quality separate from retailer/channel trust when they are different entities.
- Emit using `vendor_research_template.md`.

## Next expansion
Migrate the useful vendor-ecosystem, portfolio, support, reputation, and sourcing rules from the legacy workflow.

## Boundary
Offer-level seller risk remains owned by `seller_instructions.md`; product classification remains owned by `product_hierarchy.md`.
