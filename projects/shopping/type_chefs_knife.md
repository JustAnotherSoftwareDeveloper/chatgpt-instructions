# Type Authority: Chef's Knife

## Purpose
Own behavior specific to Chef's Knives when the `Chef's Knife` Type is active.

## Initial scope
Future rules may cover primary-knife versatility, useful blade lengths, profile, heel clearance, tip geometry, rocking vs push/pull cutting, food release, weight/balance, board/hand/use-case fit, the chef's-knife/gyuto relationship, and when another kitchen-knife Type may be a better fit.

Use the Chef's Knife sections of `criteria.md`, `source_playbooks.md`, and `pricing.md` alongside this file.

## Next expansion
Turn these concerns into concrete research and recommendation directives after the Category-level knife model is established.

## Boundary
Shared knife metallurgy, sharpening, construction, and maintenance behavior belongs to `category_kitchen_knives.md` unless genuinely Chef's-Knife-specific.
