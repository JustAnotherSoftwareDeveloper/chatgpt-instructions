# Source Playbooks

## Purpose
Define generic source-discovery semantics and the General Shopping discovery playbook.

Evidence admissibility, independence, weighting, coverage, comparability, and stopping rules remain owned by `research_sources.md`.

Domain-specific discovery guidance belongs in the active entries of `class.md`, `category.md`, and `type.md` as named Source Playbooks contributions under the composition rules in `product_hierarchy.md`.

## Standard playbook shape
A domain-specific Source Playbooks contribution may cover, where applicable:
- primary/documentary sources;
- method/test sources;
- practitioner/expert sources;
- owner/community populations;
- repair/service sources;
- current-market/seller sources;
- known source traps;
- useful search/query families;
- domain-native source archetypes that should be represented in a deep research set.

A contribution may omit source families that genuinely do not exist or add value.

## How to use
For the resolved product context:
1. Start with General Shopping.
2. Merge Source Playbooks contributions from the active Class -> Category -> Type registry entries.
3. Use the combined playbook to widen discovery before narrowing.
4. Prefer category-native expertise over generic listicles when available.
5. Ensure the research brief's primary differentiators have plausible source paths before deep collection.
6. Evaluate discovered sources under `research_sources.md`, `reviews.md`, and `seller_instructions.md` as applicable.

Discovery guidance identifies promising source families and search paths. It does not itself decide whether a discovered source can support a claim.

## General Shopping
### Primary / documentary
Look for:
- manufacturer manuals/spec sheets/compatibility/support/warranty/revision notes;
- standards, certification, regulatory, recall, or official registry sources when applicable.

### Independent empirical
Look for:
- publications/specialists with transparent methods;
- competent reviewers with direct comparisons and concrete observations;
- measured testing when the claim is measurable;
- side-by-side/common-method comparisons when ranking products directly.

### Practitioner / expert
Look for domain-native operators, repairers, installers, makers, engineers, instructors, or other practitioners when their experience maps to the product.

### Owner / community
Look for:
- long-term owner reports;
- specialist forums/communities;
- repair/troubleshooting communities;
- recurring complaints/praise across independent populations.

### Current market
When current price/availability matters, look for:
- manufacturer/direct listings;
- reputable specialist retailers;
- major retailers/marketplaces;
- enough independent offers to identify normal street pricing and anomalies;
- historical/recurring sale evidence when buy-now-vs-wait or tier placement depends on it.

### Default query families
For Standard open-market research, adapt at least these query intents:
1. `<category/model> official specs manual warranty`
2. `<category/model> test review comparison`
3. `<category/model> long term problems owner experience`
4. `<category/model> alternatives comparison`
5. `<category/model> repair service failure teardown` when serviceability/reliability matters
6. `<category/model> price history sale discontinued` when price-state modeling matters

Active hierarchy contributions should add domain-specific terms rather than replacing these blindly.

### Low-value discovery patterns
De-prioritize:
- generic "best X" affiliate pages without original work;
- copied/syndicated roundups;
- retailer marketing disguised as independent comparison;
- snippets/summaries used instead of underlying sources;
- sources that do not identify the exact model/version when identity matters.

## Boundary
This file owns generic discovery semantics only. Domain-specific discovery belongs to active hierarchy registry entries; `research_sources.md` determines evidence sufficiency/comparability; `reviews.md` interprets review/community content; `seller_instructions.md` evaluates seller/channel evidence.
