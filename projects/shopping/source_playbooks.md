# Source Playbooks

## Purpose
Define where to look for category-appropriate information. This file guides discovery only.

Evidence admissibility, independence, weighting, coverage, and stopping rules remain owned by `research_sources.md`.

## Standard playbook shape
Each hierarchy-specific playbook should cover, where applicable:
- primary/documentary sources;
- method/test sources;
- practitioner/expert sources;
- owner/community populations;
- repair/service sources;
- current-market/seller sources;
- known source traps;
- useful search/query families.

A hierarchy section may omit source families that genuinely do not exist or add value.

## How to use
For the resolved product context:
1. Start with General Shopping.
2. Add every named section selected by active hierarchy levels.
3. Use the combined playbook to widen discovery before narrowing.
4. Prefer category-native expertise over generic listicles when available.
5. Evaluate discovered sources under `research_sources.md`, `reviews.md`, and `seller_instructions.md` as applicable.

## General Shopping
### Primary / documentary
Look for:
- manufacturer manuals/spec sheets/compatibility/support/warranty/revision notes;
- standards, certification, regulatory, recall, or official registry sources when applicable.

### Independent empirical
Look for:
- publications/specialists with transparent methods;
- competent reviewers with direct comparisons and concrete observations;
- measured testing when the claim is measurable.

### Practitioner / expert
Look for domain-native operators, repairers, installers, makers, engineers, instructors, or other practitioners when their experience maps to the product.

### Owner / community
Look for:
- long-term owner reports;
- specialist forums/communities;
- repair/troubleshooting communities;
- recurring complaints/praise across independent populations.

### Current market
When current price/availability matters, look for:
- manufacturer/direct listings;
- reputable specialist retailers;
- major retailers/marketplaces;
- enough independent offers to identify normal street pricing and anomalies.

### Default query families
For Standard open-market research, adapt at least these query intents:
1. `<category/model> official specs manual warranty`
2. `<category/model> test review comparison`
3. `<category/model> long term problems owner experience`
4. `<category/model> alternatives comparison`

Specialized playbooks should add domain-specific terms rather than replacing these blindly.

### Low-value discovery patterns
De-prioritize:
- generic "best X" affiliate pages without original work;
- copied/syndicated roundups;
- retailer marketing disguised as independent comparison;
- snippets/summaries used instead of underlying sources;
- sources that do not identify the exact model/version when identity matters.

## Home & Kitchen
### Primary / documentary
Seek material/specification, care/cleaning, warranty, parts/consumables, and safety/compliance documentation where relevant.

### Method/test
Seek credible household-use testing, durability testing, cleaning/maintenance comparison, and actual-use ergonomics.

### Practitioner / expert
Depending on product, seek cooks, instructors, repair professionals, tradespeople, appliance technicians, or other domain practitioners.

### Owner / community
Seek long-term cleaning, maintenance, storage, durability, and household-use experience.

### Source traps
- professional/commercial workflows treated as automatically superior for home use;
- lifestyle content making material/safety claims without authoritative support;
- durability inferred from weight, price, metal construction, or "professional" branding alone.

## Kitchen Knives
Use multiple epistemic perspectives rather than many sources repeating the same steel/spec narrative.

### Primary / documentary
Seek:
- maker/manufacturer specifications and line documentation;
- stated steel, hardness, dimensions, construction, origin, warranty;
- authorized-dealer listings for exact line/SKU details unavailable elsewhere.

Treat performance claims as claims, not proof.

### Knife-specialist / technical
Seek sources that discuss actual grind, geometry, profile, balance, fit/finish, sharpening behavior, metallurgy, heat treatment, toughness, edge retention, corrosion, and QC.

Prioritize makers/sharpeners/technical specialists when they show concrete evidence and clear context.

### Culinary / practitioner
Seek experienced cooks/testers comparing knives in real prep tasks: fatigue, grip, food release, wedging, board interaction, repeated use, profile, and tip utility.

Professional preference is useful but not automatically representative of home users.

### Owner / community
Seek serious knife/sharpening communities and long-term owners discussing QC variation, chipping/rolling, corrosion, handle issues, counterfeit listings, service, and sharpening difficulty.

### Useful knife query families
Adapt queries such as:
- `<model> grind geometry choil spine review`
- `<model> sharpening edge retention chipping`
- `<model> long term owner QC`
- `<model> vs <adjacent model> gyuto chef knife`
- `<maker/line> counterfeit authorized dealer`

### Source traps
Actively watch for:
- ranking primarily by steel name;
- nominal hardness treated as proof of heat-treatment quality;
- Damascus/layer count/"Japanese steel"/"forged" standing in for cutting performance;
- retailer-generated comparisons favoring inventory;
- factory sharpness conflated with geometry or long-term edge behavior;
- edge retention conflated with toughness/sharpenability;
- many review sites repeating maker copy.

## Chef's Knife
Favor sources that discuss the knife as a primary general-purpose tool.

Seek evidence about:
- actual blade length/height;
- profile and cutting-motion fit;
- heel/knuckle clearance;
- tip geometry;
- balance/weight/grip interaction;
- grind, wedging, and food release across representative foods;
- comfort/control through extended prep;
- adjacent formats such as gyuto, santoku, nakiri, or Chinese cleaver when role fit is genuinely in question.

Useful query families:
- `<model> profile rocking push cut heel height`
- `<model> 210mm dimensions balance weight`
- `<model> food release wedging onions potatoes`
- `<model> chef knife gyuto long term review`

Avoid sources that treat all 8-inch/210 mm knives as interchangeable or infer primary-knife fit from steel/prestige alone.
