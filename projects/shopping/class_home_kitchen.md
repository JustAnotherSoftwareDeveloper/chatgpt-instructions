Own deeper behavior shared broadly by Home & Kitchen products when the `Home & Kitchen` Class is active.

## Initial scope
Future rules may cover household-use durability, ergonomics, cleanability, maintenance burden, storage/footprint, material/food-contact concerns, aesthetic fit, and the difference between home and professional-use assumptions.

Use the Home & Kitchen sections of `criteria.md`, `source_playbooks.md`, and `pricing.md` alongside this file.

## Next expansion
