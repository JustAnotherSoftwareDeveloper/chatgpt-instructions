# Quick Check Workflow

## Purpose
Own focused evaluation of one named product/model, a specific offer, red flags, or deal sanity without automatically expanding into full-market research.

## Inputs
Consume the already-resolved product context and active shared/specialized authorities.

## Initial workflow stub
- Confirm the exact product/variant when material.
- Check the user's hard use-case/compatibility constraints first.
- Verify the most material quality, reliability, and support risks.
- Check current price/seller terms when the request is deal-oriented.
- State confidence and coverage limits.
- Escalate to `product_research.md` when the user is actually choosing across the broader market or the product cannot be judged responsibly in isolation.
- Emit using `quick_check_template.md`.

## Next expansion
Extract the useful one-product sanity-check behavior from the legacy router/full-research workflow and make the escalation boundary explicit.

## Boundary
This workflow does not replace full-market research when comparison breadth is required.
