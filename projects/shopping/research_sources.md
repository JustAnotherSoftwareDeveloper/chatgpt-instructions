# Research Source Authority

## Purpose
Own evidence admissibility, source quality, weighting, recency, breadth, disagreement handling, and claim-to-evidence discipline for Shopping research.

## Initial stub
- Keep candidate discovery separate from evidence used to support conclusions.
- Match source type to claim type.
- Prefer primary/standards sources for objective specifications and compliance facts when available.
- Prefer transparent method-based evidence for empirical performance.
- Use broad owner/community evidence for reliability, long-term use, edge cases, and preference-sensitive issues.
- Treat current prices, availability, policies, versions, and support status as time-sensitive.
- State material uncertainty and source disagreement.

## Next expansion
Migrate the strongest evidence-quality, source-diversity, deduplication, and research-depth rules from the legacy Shopping implementation without changing ownership boundaries.

## Boundary
Source discovery belongs to `source_playbooks.md`; review-content interpretation belongs to `reviews.md`; seller/channel risk belongs to `seller_instructions.md`.
